# R0 rules and room-policy contract

Status: evidence inventory with owner-approved provisional chronicle policy. On October 23, 2026 the owner approved the room rules below and using the existing advertised catalog/creation allocations as documented provisional chronicle rules. This approval does not make them source-verified V5 rules. Missing ritual procedure values still require a concrete source or explicit ruling.

## Approved implementation policy

- Use America/New_York for the chronicle clock, with day from 06:00 inclusive until 18:00 exclusive.
- Day/night entry restrictions compose with existing traverse locks. Restricted rooms require an explicit reviewed character allowlist. No new implicit staff bypass is granted.
- A positive room hunting modifier reduces the resolved ground difficulty, with minimum difficulty 1. Apply the contribution once and display it.
- Haven ratings appear in explicit staff-adjudicated action summaries. Do not invent automatic bonuses, intrusion outcomes, capacity, or wards from a rating label.
- Existing advertised catalog values and creation distributions may be implemented as provisional chronicle rules. Preserve and label their source-review limitations; do not silently promote them to verified book rules.
- The existing ritual catalog comment in world/v5_data.py specifies five minutes per level, one Rouse unless the entry overrides it, Intelligence + Blood Sorcery and difficulty level + 1 unless overridden. These existing defaults are included in the approved provisional catalog policy. Additional unstated ritual-specific outcomes, ingredients, durations, or preparation stages must be explicit Storyteller adjudications, not inferred from names.
- The owner instructed continued supported repairs and affected checks without further repair-permission requests. The earlier two-repair escalation requirement no longer pauses this authorized fixing work.

## Additional directly checked rule evidence

The licensed [Roll20 V5 Free Basic Rules, Celerity](https://roll20.net/compendium/vtm/Celerity) explicitly describes Fleetness: one Rouse, scene duration, Celerity-rating bonus to noncombat Dexterity tests and one Dexterity + Athletics defense per turn. G3 should enforce that eligibility and per-turn limit rather than an unconditional defense bonus. This source does not supply Prowess, whose existing catalog behavior remains provisional.
