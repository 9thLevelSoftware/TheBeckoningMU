|[R+===================================================================+
|W|                        THIN-BLOOD VAMPIRES                        |
|[R+===================================================================+|n

Thin-bloods are vampires of the 14th to 16th Generation whose vitae is so
diluted they barely count as Kindred. Often called "Duskborn," they are
outcasts in vampire society. (V5 core p.109; V5 Quick Reference 2.0 p.2-3,
p.5, p.11.)

|WTHIN-BLOOD TRAITS:|n

|yBlood Potency 0:|n
  - Generation 14th-16th, Blood Potency 0 (QR p.3, p.15)
  - No clan, so no clan Bane or Compulsion (unless you take the Clan
    Curse flaw)
  - No predator type at creation (QR p.2)

|yDisciplines:|n
  - No starting or in-clan disciplines (QR p.2)
  - Learn |yThin-Blood Alchemy|n through the Thin-blood Alchemist merit
  - The Discipline Affinity merit gives a permanent dot in one
    discipline, never more than one

|yThin-blood merits and flaws:|n (QR p.11)
  - Take one to three thin-blood merits and the same number of
    thin-blood flaws; they cost no points (QR p.2)
  - Merits: Anarch Comrades, Camarilla Contact, Catenating Blood,
    Day Drinker, Discipline Affinity, Lifelike, Thin-blood Alchemist,
    Vampiric Resilience
  - Flaws: Baby Teeth, Bestial Temper, Branded by the Camarilla,
    Clan Curse, Dead Flesh, Mortal Frailty, Shunned by the Anarchs,
    Vitae Dependency
  - Some are exclusive: Dead Flesh rules out Lifelike, Mortal Frailty
    rules out Vampiric Resilience, Shunned by the Anarchs rules out
    Anarch Comrades

|WTHIN-BLOOD ALCHEMY:|n

See |yhelp alchemy|n for the formulas.

|WSOCIAL STANDING:|n

Thin-bloods are |rpariahs|n in vampire society:
  - Not recognized as true Kindred by most elders
  - Often hunted or exploited
  - Must prove their worth to gain any standing
