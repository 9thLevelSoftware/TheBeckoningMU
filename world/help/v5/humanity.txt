|W|                      HUMANITY SYSTEM                              |
|[R+===================================================================+|n

Humanity (0-10) is your connection to mortal life and your hold on the
Beast. New characters start at Humanity 7 (QR p.2; Ancillae at 6).

|WSTAINS|n (QR p.3)

You gain Stains when you break the Chronicle Tenets or your own
Convictions; the Storyteller says how many. Stains fill the unmarked boxes
of your Humanity tracker (10 minus your Humanity). If a Stain would
overfill the tracker, it isn't marked: you take one |wAggravated Willpower
damage|n instead, for each Stain that doesn't fit.

  Example: Humanity 8 leaves 2 boxes. With 2 Stains already marked, one
  more Stain becomes 1 Aggravated Willpower damage.

A messy critical doesn't add a Stain by itself.

While Stains fill the tracker (Degeneration) you are Impaired: -2 dice to
all tests until your Remorse test (QR p.3).

|WREMORSE|n (QR p.3)

At the end of each session, if you have Stains, make a Remorse test with
|w+remorse|n:
  1. Roll one die for each unmarked Humanity box: 10 - Humanity - Stains,
     with a minimum of one die. Remorse uses no Hunger dice.
  2. Any success: you keep your Humanity.
  3. No successes: you lose 1 Humanity.
  Either way, all your Stains are cleared.

  Example: Humanity 7 with 2 Stains rolls 10 - 7 - 2 = 1 die.

Humanity rises only at the Storyteller's discretion (core p.241); it can't
be bought with XP.

|WCONVICTIONS AND TOUCHSTONES|n (core p.172-173)

Convictions are your personal moral code. You take one to three at
creation; breaking one costs Stains. Each Conviction has a Touchstone: a
mortal (or something mortal) that anchors it. Harm to a Touchstone can
cost Stains, at the Storyteller's discretion.

|WCOMMANDS|n

|w+humanity|n                                 Humanity, Stains, Convictions, Touchstones
|w+humanity/conviction <text>|n               add a Conviction (up to 3)
|w+humanity/conviction/remove <number>|n      remove a Conviction
|w+humanity/touchstone <name>=<description>[/<conviction #>]|n
                                            add a Touchstone for a Conviction (default 1)
|w+humanity/touchstone/remove <number>|n      remove a Touchstone
|w+stain [<count>]|n                          mark Stains on yourself
|w+stain <character>=<count>|n                (staff) mark Stains on someone else
|w+remorse|n                                  make your Remorse test (once per 24 hours)
|w+remorse <character>|n                      (staff) run a character's Remorse test
