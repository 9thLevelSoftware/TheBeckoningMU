|[R+===================================================================+
|W|                        HUNTING SYSTEM                             |
|[R+===================================================================+|n

Vampires feed to lower their Hunger. There are two ways to feed: |w+hunt|n,
a single hunting roll, and staff-run hunt scenes (|w+hunt/staffed|n), which
staff finish with the |wfeed|n command.

|WHUNTING GROUNDS|n (QR p.12)

  slum      Difficulty 2   slums, Skid Row, housing projects
  bohemian  Difficulty 3   bohemian, gentrifying or blighted neighborhoods
  downtown  Difficulty 4   working-class areas, downtown, tourist districts
  suburbs   Difficulty 5   industrial districts, urban parkland, suburbs
  wealthy   Difficulty 6   wealthy neighborhoods

|WTHE HUNTING ROLL|n (V5 core p.175-178, p.307-308)

|y+hunt <ground>|n rolls your Predator Type's hunting pool, with your Hunger
dice, against the ground's Difficulty:

  Alleycat       Strength + Brawl           Osiris         Manipulation + Subterfuge
  Bagger         Intelligence + Streetwise  Sandman        Dexterity + Stealth
  Cleaver        Manipulation + Subterfuge  Scene Queen    Manipulation + Persuasion
  Consensualist  Manipulation + Persuasion  Siren          Charisma + Subterfuge
  Farmer         Composure + Animal Ken     Blood Leech    no single roll

|y+hunt <ground>=alt|n rolls your type's alternative pool where the book
gives one (Alleycat: Wits + Streetwise). There are no bonus dice. You can
hunt |wonce every 24 hours|n, success or failure, and not at all once your
Hunger is as low as feeding without a kill can take it; staff-run scenes
are the way to feed more. A Blood Leech, or a character without a Predator
Type (thin-bloods, fledglings), has no hunting roll: ask for a hunt scene
with +hunt/staffed. |y+huntinfo|n shows your roll.

|WFEEDING|n (QR p.12)

On a win you feed. From a human you take the most you can without harming
them, which slakes |w2 Hunger|n, and their Resonance becomes yours (only
when the hunt slakes Hunger). A Farmer
feeds on an animal (1 Hunger) and a Bagger on a blood bag (1 Hunger);
neither carries a Resonance.

  - Only draining and killing a human takes Hunger to 0. Feeding without a
    kill stops at Hunger 1 (at Blood Potency 5-7 at 2, at 8-10 at 3).
  - High Blood Potency slakes less: animal and bagged blood slake half at
    BP 2 (the half point is kept and counts with the next half) and nothing
    above it, and from BP 4 each human slakes 1-3 less. Animal Succulence
    adds 1 to animal blood and counts Blood Potency two lower for it.

The vessel's Resonance and any complication on a messy critical or
bestial failure are rolled for you as a game convention; the Storyteller
decides what a complication means.

|WSTAFF-RUN HUNTS|n

|y+hunt/staffed <ground>|n opens a Hunt Scenes job. Staff run the scene and
record the feeding with |yfeed <character>=<source>[/<resonance>[/<intensity>]]|n
(sources: small animals, animal, large animal, bag, sip, drink,
harmful <1-4>, kill). Only staff can use feed. Staff can let a character
hunt again before the 24 hours are up with |y+hunt/reset <character>|n.

|WHERD|n

With the Herd background you can slake up to your Herd dots of Hunger once
a week with no hunting roll (+background); the same Hunger floor applies.

|ySee also:|n help hunger-guide, help rouse, help frenzy-guide, help backgrounds-guide, help v5-rules-guide

|WOPT-IN AI STORY HUNT (OPTIONAL)|n

The story switch adds generated prose around the normal deterministic hunt, so
the usual dice and Hunger rules still apply. Provider output cannot alter that
result or other character state. A configured provider is required; if it is
unavailable, use ordinary |w+hunt|n instead.

|ySyntax:|n
  |w+hunt/story opt-in|n                         - Enable story requests for this character
  |w+hunt/story start <location> [persona #id]|n - Resolve a hunt and start/resume a story
  |w+hunt/story resume [action]|n                - Continue an active story
  |w+hunt/story <action>|n                       - Submit an action to the active story
  |w+hunt/story status|n                        - Show your stored hunt result without AI
  |w+hunt/story complete|n                       - Close the active story
  |w+hunt/story opt-out|n                        - Withdraw consent and close the story

AI requests contain your submitted action and only approved, authorized scene
facts. Generated prose is not a rules ruling or public Chronicle entry. Staff
must approve any memory fact or public story separately.

The original dice, pool modifiers, outcome, Hunger, Resonance and complications
appear before a narration request. Failed or timed-out prose does not undo or
repeat that hunt. Repeated start/resume requests continue the stored scene;
they never roll again, feed again or reset the hunt cooldown. Status requires
the character's owning account and works even with AI disabled or consent
withdrawn. Older saved hunts show their available facts and label original
dice unavailable; missing dice are never reconstructed or supplied by AI.
