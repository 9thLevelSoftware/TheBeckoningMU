|[R+===================================================================+
|W|                   ACTIVE EFFECTS COMMANDS                        |
|[R+===================================================================+|n

|y+effects|n  (also |y+fx|n, |y+activeeffects|n)
  List the discipline effects active on you: each effect's ID, power,
  discipline, duration and when it started.

  |yExample output:|n
    [a3f2] Cat's Grace (Celerity)
      Duration: Active until end of scene

|WSTAFF SWITCHES|n (Builder and above)

|y+effects/clear <effect id>|n   remove one of your own effects
|y+effects/clear all|n           remove all your own effects
|y+effects/tick|n                count down your turn-based effects by one
                               turn; those that reach 0 are removed

These act on the staff member's own character only.

Effects are created by the |ypower|n command; see |yhelp effects-guide|n for
what is tracked and what the Storyteller applies.

|ySee also:|n help effects-guide, help discipline_commands, help combat
