Characters are created on the game website, not in the game.

  1. Log in to the website with your game account.
  2. Open the character creation form at |y/character-creation/|n
     (linked from the website's front page).
  3. Build your character and submit it.
  4. Staff review the submission. You are notified in game when it
     is approved, or when it needs changes and can be resubmitted.

Once your character is approved, connect to the game, and use
|yic <name>|n to start playing.

For the setting and the V5 rules behind the choices on the form, see
|yhelp clans|n, |yhelp disciplines|n and |yhelp v5|n.
