|W|                    VAMPIRE DISCIPLINES                            |
|[R+===================================================================+|n

Disciplines are supernatural powers granted by vampire blood. Each clan
has three |yin-clan|n disciplines that are cheaper to learn. Caitiff have no
in-clan disciplines, and thin-bloods have none at all (they can learn
Thin-Blood Alchemy through the Thin-blood Alchemist merit).

|WCORE DISCIPLINES:|n (V5 core p.244-288)

|yAnimalism|n - Command animals and the Beast within
  - In-clan: |wGangrel, Nosferatu|n

|yAuspex|n - Heightened senses and extrasensory perception
  - In-clan: |wMalkavian, Toreador, Tremere|n

|yBlood Sorcery|n - Blood magic; its rituals are learned separately
  - In-clan: |wTremere|n

|yCelerity|n - Supernatural speed and reflexes
  - In-clan: |wBrujah, Toreador|n

|yDominate|n - Mental control and mesmerism
  - In-clan: |wMalkavian, Tremere, Ventrue|n

|yFortitude|n - Supernatural resilience and toughness
  - In-clan: |wGangrel, Ventrue|n

|yObfuscate|n - Supernatural concealment and stealth
  - In-clan: |wMalkavian, Nosferatu|n

|yPotence|n - Supernatural strength
  - In-clan: |wBrujah, Nosferatu|n

|yPresence|n - Supernatural charisma and emotional manipulation
  - In-clan: |wBrujah, Toreador, Ventrue|n

|yProtean|n - Shapeshifting and body transformation
  - In-clan: |wGangrel|n

|yThin-Blood Alchemy|n - Formulas distilled from blood and emotion
  - Thin-bloods only, through the Thin-blood Alchemist merit

|WUSING DISCIPLINES:|n

Many discipline powers cost a |yRouse Check|n; some cost two or more, and
some are free. See |yhelp disciplines_powers|n for every power's level and
cost.
  - Roll 1d10
  - Success (6+): No Hunger increase
  - Failure (1-5): |[RHunger increases by 1|n

|WLEARNING DISCIPLINES:|n (V5 Quick Reference 2.0 p.1)

  - In-clan discipline: |ynew level x 5 XP|n
  - Out-of-clan discipline: |ynew level x 7 XP|n
  - Caitiff discipline: |ynew level x 6 XP|n
  - Blood Sorcery ritual or thin-blood formula: |ylevel x 3 XP|n

|yAmalgam Powers:|n
  - Require a second discipline at a set level
  - Example: |yUnerring Aim|n (Celerity 4) requires Auspex 2
