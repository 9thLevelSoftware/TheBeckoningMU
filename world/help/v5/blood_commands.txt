|W|                    BLOOD SYSTEM COMMANDS                         |
|[R+===================================================================+|n

Commands for managing vampire blood mechanics including feeding,
Blood Surge, and Hunger tracking.

|WFEEDING:|n

Players feed with |y+hunt <hunting ground>|n, or ask for a staff-run hunt
scene with |y+hunt/staffed <ground>|n (see help hunt-guide).

|yfeed <character>=<source>[/<resonance>[/<intensity>]]|n   (staff only)
  Record a feeding from a staff-run scene. Sources (QR p.12): small
  animals, animal, large animal, bag, sip, drink, harmful <1-4>, kill.
  Only a kill takes Hunger to 0; Blood Potency feeding penalties apply.

|yExamples:|n
  feed Bob=drink/choleric
  feed Bob=harmful 3/sanguine/2
  feed Bob=kill

|WBLOOD SURGE:|n

|ybloodsurge <attribute or physical skill>|n
|ysurge <attribute or physical skill>|n
  Surge your blood to add dice to your next roll.

|yExamples:|n
  bloodsurge strength            # Then: roll 5 (rolls 5 + surge dice)
  surge brawl

|yEffects:|n
  - Adds dice by |WBlood Potency|n: BP 0 +1, BP 1-2 +2, BP 3-4 +3,
    BP 5-6 +4, BP 7-8 +5, BP 9-10 +6
  - The dice go on your next |yroll|n or |ypower|n roll only, then the
    surge is used up; an unused surge lapses after an hour and costs
    nothing. One surge at a time.
  - Costs one |WRouse check|n, made with the surged roll: the roll uses
    your Hunger from before it, and a failure raises Hunger afterwards.
    Refused at Hunger 5.

|yValid Attributes:|n
  Strength, Dexterity, Stamina, Charisma, Manipulation,
  Composure, Intelligence, Wits, Resolve

|yValid Physical Skills:|n
  Athletics, Brawl, Craft, Drive, Firearms, Larceny,
  Melee, Stealth, Survival

|WHUNGER STATUS:|n

|yhunger|n
  View your current Hunger level and blood status.

|yDisplays:|n
  - Current Hunger level (0-5)
  - Visual Hunger bar
  - Hunger effects
  - Current resonance (if any)
  - Blood Surge status (if active)

|WHUNGER 5:|n

At |[RHunger 5|n you can't Rouse the Blood: |yrouse|n, |ybloodsurge|n,
mending with |y+heal|n and any power that needs a Rouse check are refused
until you feed. What Hunger dice do to your rolls (messy criticals and
bestial failures) is in |yhelp hunger-guide|n and |yhelp v5-rules-guide|n.
