|[R+===================================================================+
|W|                       VAMPIRE CLANS                               |
|[R+===================================================================+|n

Each vampire belongs to a |yclan|n - a bloodline with distinct traits,
disciplines, and a supernatural |[Rbane|n. This game uses the clans of the
V5 core book (p.63-113). A bane's strength is your |yBane Severity|n, which
comes from your Blood Potency (BP 0: 0, BP 1-2: 2, BP 3-4: 3, BP 5-6: 4,
BP 7-8: 5, BP 9-10: 6). A |yCompulsion|n strikes on a bestial failure.

|yBrujah|n - The Rabble, rebels who rage against tyranny
  - Disciplines: |wCelerity, Potence, Presence|n
  - Bane: |[RViolent Temper|n - subtract Bane Severity dice from rolls to
    resist fury frenzy
  - Compulsion: |[RRebellion|n - -2 dice to all pools until you defy an
    order or expectation, or change someone's mind

|yGangrel|n - The Outlanders, who blend vampire and beast
  - Disciplines: |wAnimalism, Fortitude, Protean|n
  - Bane: |[RBestial Features|n - in frenzy you gain animal features equal to
    Bane Severity, each -1 to an Attribute, for one more night
  - Compulsion: |[RFeral Impulses|n - for a scene, -3 dice to Manipulation and
    Intelligence rolls, and you speak only in one-word sentences

|yMalkavian|n - The Lunatics, whose madness conceals and reveals truths
  - Disciplines: |wAuspex, Dominate, Obfuscate|n
  - Bane: |[RFractured Perspective|n - on a bestial failure or compulsion, a
    penalty equal to Bane Severity to one category of pools for the scene
  - Compulsion: |[RDelusion|n - for a scene, -2 dice to Dexterity,
    Manipulation, Composure and Wits rolls and to resist terror frenzy

|yNosferatu|n - The Sewer Rats, hideous gatherers of secrets
  - Disciplines: |wAnimalism, Obfuscate, Potence|n
  - Bane: |[RRepulsiveness|n - you take the Repulsive flaw and can't buy Looks
    merits; passing as undeformed loses Bane Severity dice
  - Compulsion: |[RCryptophilia|n - -2 dice to actions not aimed at learning
    a secret, until you learn a useful one

|yToreador|n - The Degenerates, seekers of art, romance and thrills
  - Disciplines: |wAuspex, Celerity, Presence|n
  - Bane: |[RAesthetic Fixation|n - in ugly surroundings, subtract Bane
    Severity dice from Discipline pools
  - Compulsion: |[RObsession|n - -2 dice to actions unrelated to your
    fixation, until you can't perceive it or the scene ends

|yTremere|n - The Warlocks, sorcerers rebuilding their power
  - Disciplines: |wAuspex, Blood Sorcery, Dominate|n
  - Bane: |[RDeficient Blood|n - you can't Blood Bond Kindred; mortals and
    ghouls need Bane Severity extra drinks to be bound
  - Compulsion: |[RPerfectionism|n - -2 dice to all pools until a critical win
    on a Skill roll or the scene ends

|yVentrue|n - The Blue Bloods, aristocrats who enforce the Traditions
  - Disciplines: |wDominate, Fortitude, Presence|n
  - Bane: |[RRarefied Taste|n - feeding outside your chosen prey costs
    Willpower equal to Bane Severity, or the blood won't stay down
  - Compulsion: |[RArrogance|n - -2 dice to actions not related to leadership,
    until someone obeys an order you gave without supernatural compulsion

|yCaitiff|n - The Clanless, outcasts with no common traits
  - No in-clan disciplines (choose any two at creation); disciplines cost
    new level x 6 XP
  - No bane or compulsion, but you start with the Suspect flaw

|yThin-blood|n - The Duskborn, too diluted to share the full curse
  - Generation 14th-16th, Blood Potency 0, no in-clan disciplines
  - No bane or compulsion; take thin-blood merits and flaws instead
  - See |yhelp thinblood|n

|WOTHER CLANS:|n

The Banu Haqim, Ministry, Lasombra, Hecata, Tzimisce, Ravnos and Salubri
come from later V5 books, not the core book, and are not available.

|WCHOOSING YOUR CLAN:|n

Your clan determines:
  - |yStarting disciplines|n (in-clan are cheaper)
  - |ySupernatural bane|n (permanent curse)
  - |yCompulsion|n (triggered by bestial failures)

Characters are created on the game website; see |yhelp chargen|n.
