|W|                        FRENZY SYSTEM                              |
|[R+===================================================================+|n

Frenzy is when the Beast takes control, overriding your rational mind with
primal instinct. Vampires can enter frenzy when provoked, starving, or
confronted with their banes (fire, sunlight, etc.).

|WFRENZY TYPES:|n (V5 Quick Reference 2.0 p.4, p.13)

|WFury Frenzy|n - you stop at nothing to destroy the cause of the provocation
  Friend killed 2, Lover or Touchstone hurt 3, Lover or Touchstone
  killed 4, physical provocation or harassment 2, insulted by an
  inferior 2, public humiliation 2

|WHunger Frenzy|n - you seek fresh human blood from the closest source
  Sight of an open wound or overpowering smell of blood at Hunger 4+ 2,
  taste of blood at Hunger 4+ 3, failing a Rouse check at Hunger 5 4

|WTerror Frenzy (Rotschreck)|n - you flee the danger, heedless of anything
  in your way
  Bonfire 2, being burned 2, inside a burning building 3, obscured
  sunlight (through a window) 3, fully exposed to direct sunlight 4

The number after each provocation is the Difficulty of the frenzy test.

|WRESISTING FRENZY:|n

Roll |WWillpower + Humanity / 3|n (current Willpower plus a third of your
Humanity, rounded down) against the provocation's Difficulty. If you fail,
the Storyteller takes control of you until the frenzy ends. You can spend
Willpower to regain control from a frenzy. The frenzy test is a Willpower
test: it uses |wno Hunger dice|n and no Blood Surge.

Clan banes can change the roll: a |yBrujah|n subtracts Bane Severity dice
from rolls to resist fury frenzy (see help clans).

|yExample:|n A vampire with 5 Willpower and Humanity 7 is publicly
humiliated (fury, Difficulty 2). They roll 5 + 2 = 7 dice and need two
successes.

|WHUNGER FRENZY FROM A ROUSE PAST HUNGER 5|n (core p.211)

Hunger never rises above 5. You can't choose to Rouse the Blood at Hunger 5,
but a Rouse can still be forced on you:
  - a power with two or three Rouse checks, started below Hunger 5, whose
    failed checks would take you past 5: you owe a hunger frenzy test at
    |wDifficulty 4|n for each failed check past 5 (the power still works);
  - rising for the night (|wrouse/wake|n): the Rouse is rolled even at
    Hunger 5. If it fails there, Hunger stays at 5, you make one hunger
    frenzy test at Difficulty 4 (QR p.13), and you fall into torpor (QR p.4;
    staff decide how long it lasts).
The command that caused it rolls the test(s) at once; once one fails you
are in frenzy and the rest aren't rolled. A test still owed shows in
|whunger|n and on your sheet; roll it with |w+frenzy/pending|n.

|WIN FRENZY|n

The Storyteller takes control of you (QR p.4): in |wfury|n you stop at nothing
to destroy the source of the provocation, in |whunger|n you seek fresh human
blood from the closest source, in |wterror|n you flee the danger. You can
spend Willpower to regain control (QR p.3).

|WRIDING THE WAVE|n (QR p.4)

When a frenzy test is called for you may choose not to resist and "ride the
wave": you keep control of your character but must play out the frenzy to
its end.

|WCOMMANDS|n

|w+frenzy|n                               your frenzy test pool and any test you owe
|w+frenzy/check <fury|hunger|terror>|n    the provocations and their difficulties
|w+frenzy/resist <difficulty> [<type>]|n  roll to resist (the type matters for banes)
|w+frenzy/pending|n                       roll a hunger frenzy test you owe

|WCLAN BANES & FRENZY|n (see help clans)

|WBrujah|n - Violent Temper: subtract Bane Severity dice from rolls to resist
  fury frenzy (+frenzy/resist <difficulty> fury applies it).
|WGangrel|n - Bestial Features: in frenzy, gain animal features equal to Bane
  Severity, each -1 to an Attribute, lasting one more night (only one if
  you ride the wave).
