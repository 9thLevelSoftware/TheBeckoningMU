|[R+===================================================================+
|W|                        FRENZY SYSTEM                              |
|[R+===================================================================+|n

Frenzy is when the Beast takes control, overriding your rational mind with
primal instinct. Vampires can enter frenzy when provoked, starving, or
confronted with their banes (fire, sunlight, etc.).

|WFRENZY TYPES:|n (V5 Quick Reference 2.0 p.4, p.13)

|WFury Frenzy|n - you stop at nothing to destroy the cause of the provocation
  Friend killed 2, Lover or Touchstone hurt 3, Lover or Touchstone
  killed 4, physical provocation or harassment 2, insulted by an
  inferior 2, public humiliation 2

|WHunger Frenzy|n - you seek fresh human blood from the closest source
  Sight of an open wound or overpowering smell of blood at Hunger 4+ 2,
  taste of blood at Hunger 4+ 3, failing a Rouse check at Hunger 5 4

|WTerror Frenzy (Rotschreck)|n - you flee the danger, heedless of anything
  in your way
  Bonfire 2, being burned 2, inside a burning building 3, obscured
  sunlight (through a window) 3, fully exposed to direct sunlight 4

The number after each provocation is the Difficulty of the frenzy test.

|WRESISTING FRENZY:|n

Roll |WWillpower + Humanity / 3|n (current Willpower plus a third of your
Humanity, rounded down) against the provocation's Difficulty. If you fail,
the Storyteller takes control of you until the frenzy ends. You can spend
Willpower to regain control from a frenzy.

Clan banes can change the roll: a |yBrujah|n subtracts Bane Severity dice
from rolls to resist fury frenzy (see help clans).

|yExample:|n A vampire with 5 Willpower and Humanity 7 is publicly
humiliated (fury, Difficulty 2). They roll 5 + 2 = 7 dice and need two
successes.

|WFRENZY CONSEQUENCES:|n

When you enter frenzy:

• You |Wlose control|n of your character - the ST or game system determines
  your actions
• You gain the |WBeast's Traits|n - increased physical prowess but no
  cunning
• You cannot use |WDisciplines|n that require concentration (Dominate,
  Auspex, etc.)
• You |Wmay|n use physical Disciplines automatically (Celerity, Potence)
• You gain |W1 Stain|n for actions taken during frenzy (at ST discretion)
• You may violate your |WConvictions|n or harm |WTouchstones|n, gaining more
  Stains

|WRIDING THE WAVE:|n

Instead of resisting frenzy, you can choose to |W"ride the wave"|n - you
accept the frenzy and give in to the Beast, but maintain some narrative
control over your actions.

Benefits:
• You don't need to roll to resist
• You can guide your actions somewhat (with ST approval)
• You may avoid some Stains by directing the frenzy appropriately

Drawbacks:
• You're still in frenzy - the Beast is in charge
• You must pursue the frenzy's goal (feed, fight, flee)
• You may still gain Stains for your actions

|WENDING FRENZY:|n

Frenzy typically lasts until:
• The trigger is resolved (fed enough, enemy defeated, escaped danger)
• The ST determines the scene has ended
• Another vampire uses |WDominate|n to snap you out of it
• You fulfill the Beast's desire

After frenzy ends, you regain control and must deal with the consequences
of your actions.

|WCOMMANDS:|n

|W+frenzy|n
  Check your current frenzy risk based on Humanity and Hunger

|W+frenzy/check <type>|n
  Assess frenzy risk for a specific trigger (hunger, fury, terror)
  Example: |W+frenzy/check hunger|n

|W+frenzy/resist <difficulty>|n
  Roll to resist frenzy against a Difficulty
  Example: |W+frenzy/resist 4|n

|WCLAN BANES & FRENZY:|n (V5 core clan chapter; see help clans)

|WBrujah|n - Violent Temper
  Subtract Bane Severity dice from rolls to resist fury frenzy

|WGangrel|n - Bestial Features
  In frenzy, gain animal features equal to Bane Severity, each -1 to an
  Attribute, lasting one more night (only one if you ride the wave)
