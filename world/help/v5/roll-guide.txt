|[R+===================================================================+
|W|                    DICE ROLLING MECHANICS                         |
|[R+===================================================================+|n

V5 uses |yd10 dice pools|n to resolve actions (Quick Reference p.3-4).

|WBASIC ROLL STRUCTURE:|n

Roll |yAttribute + Skill|n dice; you need a number of successes equal to
the |yDifficulty|n the Storyteller sets.

|yExample:|n Sneak past a guard
  - Pool: |wDexterity 3 + Stealth 2 = 5 dice|n
  - Difficulty: |w2|n
  - Command: |yroll 5 vs 2|n

|WSUCCESSES:|n

  - Each die showing |w6-10|n is |w1 success|n; 1-5 is none.
  - Each |gpair of 10s|n is worth |g4 successes|n in total.
    One 10 = 1 success, two = 4, three = 5, four = 8.
  - You succeed if your successes reach the difficulty. At difficulty
    0 you still need one success.

|yExample:|n You roll [3, 6, 7, 10, 10]
  - 6 and 7 are 2 successes; the pair of 10s is 4. Total |g6|n.

|yCritical:|n a successful roll with at least one pair of 10s.

|WHUNGER DICE:|n

Your |[RHunger dice|n replace regular dice, one per point of Hunger,
up to the whole pool. |yroll|n uses your current Hunger automatically.

|yExample:|n 5 dice at Hunger 2 = |W3 regular dice|n + |[R2 Hunger dice|n.
At Hunger 4, a 3-die pool is all Hunger dice.

|WMESSY CRITICAL:|n

A critical where |[Ra Hunger die shows one of the 10s|n. You succeed,
but the Beast shows: the Storyteller decides the complication.

|WBESTIAL FAILURE:|n

A failed roll with |[Rany Hunger die showing 1|n. You fail, and you
act out a Compulsion.

|WWILLPOWER:|n

Mark 1 Superficial Willpower damage to re-roll up to |g3 regular dice|n
of your last roll. You choose them by the value they show, and you may
re-roll a 10. Hunger dice can't be re-rolled.

  |yroll/willpower 2 3|n      re-roll a 2 and a 3 from your last roll

|WBLOOD SURGE:|n

|ybloodsurge <trait>|n adds the Blood Potency table's surge dice to your
next |yroll|n or |ypower|n roll. Its Rouse check is made with that roll, and
any Hunger it costs is added after the roll (see |yhelp bloodsurge|n).

|WCOMMANDS:|n

|yroll <pool> [vs <difficulty>]|n      roll with your Hunger
|yroll/secret <pool> [vs <difficulty>]|n  only you see it
|yroll/willpower <die> [<die> <die>]|n  Willpower re-roll
|ypower <power name>|n                 use a discipline power
|yrouse|n                              a Rouse check

|WROLEPLAYING ROLLS:|n

|yDescribe first, then roll.|n Narrate how you succeed or what goes
wrong; on a messy critical or bestial failure, play out the Beast.
