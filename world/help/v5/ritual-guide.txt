|WRITUAL CASTING — PROVISIONAL CHRONICLE PROCEDURE|n

|y+ritual|n lists learned rituals.
|y+ritual <name> [= <target>]|n creates an UNPAID request.
|y+ritual/status [<cast-id>]|n shows durable state, revision, payments and dice.
|y+ritual/confirm <cast-id>|n confirms the attested procedure and its costs.
|y+ritual/resolve <cast-id>|n checks a due casting explicitly (also timed by its Script).
|y+ritual/cancel <cast-id>|n cancels an ordinary waiting attempt; recorded payments remain paid.

You must have learned the ritual and meet its Blood Sorcery rating. The
catalog's provisional defaults are five minutes per level, one Rouse check
unless the entry overrides it, Intelligence + Blood Sorcery and difficulty
level + 1 unless overridden. Truth of Blood and Dagon's Call retain their
Resolve pools and catalog resistance pools. No Discipline-power Blood
Potency reroll, surge, or other bonus is silently applied to ritual rolls.

The catalog does not supply every target fact, ingredient, payment timing,
cancellation consequence, opposed procedure, duration or outcome. There is
no inventory model proving materials. An authenticated Builder must attest
these facts for the EXACT request revision, then you confirm that procedure.
Payment may precede casting or follow its roll only as explicitly attested;
cancellation/failure retain paid costs and grant no automatic effect.
Rouse failures can raise Hunger. Hunger 5 refuses unpaid voluntary checks.
Pending Hunger frenzy tests remain recorded and visible; use +frenzy.

A successful CASTING ROLL is not automatically a discovered identity,
truth, ward, transport, damage, immunity or other supernatural outcome.
It waits for staff adjudication. A staff-adjudicated outcome can add only
a descriptive +effects marker with an explicit duration; it is not an
automatic mechanical power. Staff tick advances attested turn counters;
scene/night/permanent markers require explicit Storyteller removal.

Escape to True Sanctuary's twelve Rouse checks are spread over preparation.
Staff must name twelve distinct one-check checkpoints, record their facts
and attest that the catalog's 25-minute casting follows their completion.
Each checkpoint requires its own staff confirmation/payment. No preparation
interval is guessed, no remaining checks are charged by a loop, and feeding
between checkpoints does not cause a completed checkpoint to charge again.

The attempt, exact dice, procedure, payment ledger and history survive
reloads. Waiting casting deadlines resume. Interrupted payment, resolution
or adjudication becomes review_required on startup and NEVER automatically
recharges, rerolls or repeats an effect. Status does not declare an operation
stale while it may still be running. Staff review can explicitly abandon an
investigated attempt, preserving its uncertainty/history without refund or
replay. Any resource correction is a separate staff responsibility.

|WBUILDER PROCEDURES|n

All actions are bound to the requested character and exact cast revision:

|y+ritual/attest <character>/<cast-id> = <revision>/<JSON procedure>|n
|y+ritual/pay <character>/<cast-id> = <revision>/<checkpoint-id>|n
|y+ritual/adjudicate <character>/<cast-id> = <revision>/<duration>/<outcome>|n
|y+ritual/review <character>/<cast-id> = <revision>/<investigation note>|n

An ordinary attestation object must explicitly provide:
{"materials":"witnessed materials and preparation", "target_facts":"verified facts",
 "duration":"staff_adjudicated", "outcome":"requested adjudication",
 "failure":"no effect; paid costs retained", "failure_policy":"no_effect_retain_paid",
 "cancellation":"retain_paid", "payment_timing":"before_casting",
 "casting_start":"after_payment", "casting_relationship":"recorded preparation/time facts",
 "opposition":"none", "opposed_ties":"defender_wins",
 "opposed_success":"catalog_difficulty_and_contest", "checkpoints":[]}

These are required statements, not facts automatically supplied by the game.
For after_casting payment use casting_start on_confirm. Catalog opposition
requires opposition catalog_roll and an explicitly selected actor_wins or
defender_wins tie policy, plus opposed_success catalog_difficulty_and_contest
to attest that BOTH the catalog difficulty and contest apply. Use
staff_adjudicated opposition for another procedure. Sanctuary requires
payment_timing staged_preparation, casting_start after_checkpoints and twelve
objects such as {"id":"prep-1","rouse":1,"description":"verified checkpoint facts"}.
Adjudication durations: instant, scene, night, permanent, or turn:<positive count>.

All catalog values remain provisional chronicle policy pending source review.
