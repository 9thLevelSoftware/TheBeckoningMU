# Disciplines and Powers

## Overview

Disciplines are supernatural powers fueled by vampiric blood. Each discipline has powers at levels 1-5. Using a discipline power typically requires a Rouse check, which may increase your Hunger.

## Commands

### View Your Disciplines
```
+disciplines
+disc
```
Shows all your known disciplines and their powers.

### View Specific Discipline
```
+disciplines <discipline name>
+disc animalism
```
Shows all powers for a specific discipline you know.

### Get Power Information
```
+powerinfo <discipline>/<power name>
+pinfo celerity/cat's grace
```
Shows detailed information about a specific discipline power.

### Activate a Power
```
+power <discipline>/<power name>
+activate animalism/bond famulus
+use dominate/compel
```
Activates a discipline power. If the power requires a Rouse check, it will be performed automatically.

## The Core Disciplines

The V5 core book's disciplines (V5 core p.244-288). Each power is listed as
name (level, cost). Amalgam powers also need the second discipline.

### Animalism
Commune with and command animals and the Beast (V5 core p.244-247).

- Bond Famulus (1, 3 Rouse): Ghoul one animal as a bonded companion that obeys simple commands
- Sense the Beast (1, free): Sense hostility, Hunger and supernatural nature in others
- Feral Whispers (2, 1 Rouse): Talk with animals and summon those nearby
- Animal Succulence (3, free): Feeding on animals slakes more Hunger
- Quell the Beast (3, 1 Rouse): Cow a mortal into apathy, or pull a vampire out of frenzy
- Unliving Hive (3, free; needs Obfuscate 2): Your body hosts insect swarms that your Animalism powers can command
- Subsume the Spirit (4, 1 Rouse): Move your mind into an animal's body and control it
- Animal Dominion (5, 2 Rouse): Command whole flocks or swarms of animals
- Drawing Out the Beast (5, 1 Rouse): Push your own frenzy out onto a nearby victim

### Auspex
Supernatural senses and perception (V5 core p.248-252).

- Heightened Senses (1, free): Add Auspex to perception rolls; senses sharpen beyond human limits
- Sense the Unseen (1, free): Notice Obfuscated beings, ghosts and spying Auspex
- Premonition (2, free): Receive intuitive visions and warnings
- Scry the Soul (3, 1 Rouse): Read a subject's aura: emotions, nature, diablerie
- Share the Senses (3, 1 Rouse): See and hear through another person's senses
- Spirit's Touch (4, 1 Rouse): Read emotional traces left on objects and places
- Clairvoyance (5, 1 Rouse): Gather information about the surrounding area
- Possession (5, 2 Rouse; needs Dominate 3): Take over a mortal's body
- Telepathy (5, 1 Rouse): Read surface thoughts or send your own

### Blood Sorcery
Blood magic and rituals (V5 core p.271-282).

- Corrosive Vitae (1, 1 Rouse): Your blood eats through inanimate, non-living matter
- A Taste for Blood (1, free): Tasting a drop of blood reveals basic facts about its owner
- Extinguish Vitae (2, 1 Rouse): Spoil another vampire's blood, raising their Hunger
- Blood of Potency (3, 1 Rouse): Temporarily raise your own Blood Potency
- Scorpion's Touch (3, 1 Rouse): Turn your blood into a paralysing poison
- Theft of Vitae (4, 1 Rouse): Draw a mortal's blood through the air into your mouth
- Baal's Caress (5, 1 Rouse): Turn your blood into a poison that deals Aggravated damage
- Cauldron of Blood (5, 1 Rouse): Boil a victim's blood inside their body

Rituals (bought separately; 1 Rouse, Intelligence + Blood Sorcery):
- Level 1: Blood Walk, Clinging of the Insect, Craft Bloodstone, Wake with Evening's Freshness, Ward against Ghouls
- Level 2: Communicate with Kindred Sire, Eyes of Babel, Illuminate the Trail of Prey, Truth of Blood, Ward against Spirits, Warding Circle against Ghouls
- Level 3: Dagon's Call, Deflection of Wooden Doom, Essence of Air, Firewalker, Ward against Lupines, Warding Circle against Spirits
- Level 4: Defense of the Sacred Haven, Eyes of the Nighthawk, Incorporeal Passage, Ward against Cainites, Warding Circle against Lupines
- Level 5: Escape to True Sanctuary, Heart of Stone, Shaft of Belated Dissolution, Warding Circle against Cainites

### Celerity
Supernatural speed and reflexes (V5 core p.252-254).

- Cat's Grace (1, free): Automatically keep your balance
- Rapid Reflexes (1, free): Dodge gunfire without cover at no penalty; minor actions are faster
- Fleetness (2, 1 Rouse): Add Celerity to non-combat Dexterity rolls and to Dexterity-based defense
- Blink (3, 1 Rouse): Dash a long distance almost instantly and still act
- Traversal (3, 1 Rouse): Run up walls or across liquid
- Draught of Elegance (4, 1 Rouse): Those who drink your blood gain Celerity
- Unerring Aim (4, 1 Rouse; needs Auspex 2): The target can't defend against your ranged attack
- Lightning Strike (5, 1 Rouse): The target can't defend against your close-combat attack
- Split Second (5, 1 Rouse): Act in a sudden moment, rewriting what just happened

### Dominate
Mind control and mental commands (V5 core p.254-257).

- Cloud Memory (1, free): Make the target forget the current moment
- Compel (1, free): A one-word or short command the target obeys at once
- Mesmerize (2, 1 Rouse): Implant a more complex command
- Dementation (2, 1 Rouse; needs Obfuscate 2): Push the target toward breakdown or madness
- The Forgetful Mind (3, 1 Rouse): Rewrite the target's memories
- Submerged Directive (3, free): Mesmerize commands can wait for a trigger
- Rationalize (4, free): Victims explain away dominated acts as their own choice
- Mass Manipulation (5, 1 Rouse): Use a Dominate power on a whole group at once
- Terminal Decree (5, free): Your commands may now harm or kill the victim

### Fortitude
Supernatural resilience and toughness (V5 core p.258-260).

- Resilience (1, free): Add Fortitude to your Health track
- Unswayable Mind (1, free): Add Fortitude to resist coercion and attempts to sway your mind
- Toughness (2, 1 Rouse): Subtract Fortitude from Superficial physical damage
- Enduring Beasts (2, 1 Rouse; needs Animalism 1): Animals you influence gain extra Health equal to your Fortitude
- Defy Bane (3, 1 Rouse): Turn Aggravated damage from your banes into Superficial
- Fortify the Inner Facade (3, free): Harder to read with Scry the Soul or Telepathy; add Fortitude to resist
- Draught of Endurance (4, 1 Rouse): Those who drink your blood temporarily gain Fortitude
- Flesh of Marble (5, 2 Rouse): Ignore the first source of physical damage each turn (not sunlight)
- Prowess from Pain (5, 1 Rouse): No wound penalties; your injuries boost physical Attributes

### Obfuscate
Supernatural stealth and invisibility (V5 core p.260-263).

- Cloak of Shadows (1, free): Become unseen while you keep still
- Silence of Death (1, free): Silence every sound you make
- Unseen Passage (2, 1 Rouse): Stay hidden while you move
- Ghost in the Machine (3, free): Your Obfuscate also fools cameras, recordings and electronics
- Mask of a Thousand Faces (3, 1 Rouse): Appear as a forgettable stranger who fits the setting
- Conceal (4, 1 Rouse; needs Auspex 3): Hide an object up to the size of a small building
- Vanish (4, free): Use Cloak of Shadows or Unseen Passage while being watched
- Cloak the Gathering (5, 1 Rouse): Extend your Obfuscate to your companions
- Impostor's Guise (5, 1 Rouse): Look like one specific person

### Potence
Supernatural strength (V5 core p.263-266).

- Lethal Body (1, free): Your unarmed blows deal Aggravated damage to mortals
- Soaring Leap (1, free): Jump much higher and farther
- Prowess (2, 1 Rouse): Add Potence to unarmed and melee damage and to Strength feats
- Brutal Feed (3, free): Drain a mortal completely within seconds
- Spark of Rage (3, 1 Rouse; needs Presence 3): Stir onlookers to anger or frenzy
- Uncanny Grip (3, 1 Rouse): Climb and hang from walls and ceilings
- Draught of Might (4, 1 Rouse): Those who drink your blood temporarily gain Potence
- Earthshock (5, 2 Rouse): Strike the ground to stagger or knock down those nearby
- Fist of Caine (5, 1 Rouse): Your unarmed attacks deal Aggravated damage to mortals and vampires

### Presence
Supernatural charisma and emotion manipulation (V5 core p.266-269).

- Awe (1, free): Become captivating; add Presence to Persuasion, Performance and Charisma pools
- Daunt (1, free): Add Presence to Intimidation; can't be used with Awe
- Lingering Kiss (2, free): Those you feed on grow attached to you
- Dread Gaze (3, 1 Rouse): Terrify a target into fleeing or cowering
- Entrancement (3, 1 Rouse): The target becomes infatuated and eager to please
- Irresistible Voice (4, free; needs Dominate 1): Use Dominate with your voice alone, without eye contact
- Summon (4, 1 Rouse): Call someone you have used Presence on, or who tasted your blood
- Majesty (5, 2 Rouse): No one can act against you without first resisting
- Star Magnetism (5, 1 Rouse): Presence powers work through live video and audio

### Protean
Shapeshifting and transformation (V5 core p.269-271).

- Eyes of the Beast (1, free): Your eyes glow and see in total darkness
- Weight of the Feather (1, free): Become nearly weightless; no damage from falls or impacts
- Feral Weapons (2, 1 Rouse): Grow deadly claws or fangs
- Earth Meld (3, 1 Rouse): Sink into the earth to rest
- Shapechange (3, 1 Rouse): Turn into one human-sized animal
- Metamorphosis (4, 1 Rouse): Take an extra animal form of any size
- Mist Form (5, 1 Rouse): Turn into mist that only fire, sun or supernatural attacks can harm
- The Unfettered Heart (5, free): Your heart moves within your chest, making you much harder to stake

### Thin-Blood Alchemy
Alchemical formulae unique to thin-blooded vampires (V5 core p.282-288).

Thin-bloods get Alchemy through the Thin-blood Alchemist merit. Formulas:
- Far Reach (1): Telekinetically shove or hold an object or person
- Haze (1): Surround yourself with a cloud of mist
- Envelop (2): A mist smothers and blinds one target
- Counterfeit (2): Copy a Discipline power one level below your Alchemy rating
- Defractionate (3): Make bagged or treated blood drinkable and nourishing
- Profane Hieros Gamos (3): Permanently reshape your body or sex
- Airborne Momentum (4): Fly under your own power
- Awaken the Sleeper (5): Wake a vampire from day-sleep or torpor

## Rouse Checks

Most discipline powers require a Rouse check. When you activate such a power:
- Roll a single d10
- Success (6+): Hunger stays the same
- Failure (1-5): Hunger increases by 1

Some powers cost two or more Rouse checks; the list above shows the cost.
At Hunger 5 you can't make a Rouse check (QR p.4). Blood Potency lets you
re-roll a failed Rouse check for low-level powers (see help hunger).

The power activates regardless of the Rouse check result, but failure means you're closer to losing control.

## Amalgam Powers

Some powers combine two disciplines (V5 core p.244). You must have both
disciplines at the required levels:
- Unliving Hive (Animalism 3) requires Obfuscate 2
- Possession (Auspex 5) requires Dominate 3
- Unerring Aim (Celerity 4) requires Auspex 2
- Dementation (Dominate 2) requires Obfuscate 2
- Enduring Beasts (Fortitude 2) requires Animalism 1
- Conceal (Obfuscate 4) requires Auspex 3
- Spark of Rage (Potence 3) requires Presence 3
- Irresistible Voice (Presence 4) requires Dominate 1

## Resonance Bonuses

The blood you last drank carries a Resonance (V5 core p.226-231). Intense or
Acute Resonance adds one die to the matching disciplines' pools; Fleeting
Resonance adds no dice but lets you spend XP on them:

- **Choleric** (angry, violent, bullying, passionate, envious): Celerity, Potence
- **Melancholy** (sad, scared, intellectual, depressed, grounded): Fortitude, Obfuscate
- **Phlegmatic** (lazy, apathetic, calm, controlling, sentimental): Auspex, Dominate
- **Sanguine** (horny, happy, addicted, active, flighty, enthusiastic): Blood Sorcery, Presence
- **Animal blood**: Animalism, Protean (bagged blood: none)

## Learning Disciplines

Disciplines are typically learned during character creation based on your clan, or purchased with XP using the +spend command.

See also: +help xp, +help clans, +help hunger

View all your disciplines:
```
+disciplines
```

View only your Animalism powers:
```
+disc animalism
```

Get info about a specific power:
```
+powerinfo celerity/cat's grace
```

Use a discipline power:
```
+power animalism/sense the beast
+activate dominate/compel
+use celerity/fleetness
```
