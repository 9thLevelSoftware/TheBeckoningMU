|[R+===================================================================+
|W|                     DISCIPLINE EFFECTS                            |
|[R+===================================================================+|n

Some discipline powers last beyond the moment you use them. When a
|ypower|n roll succeeds (or a power with no roll is used), the game records
the power as an |wactive effect|n on you, so you and the Storyteller can see
what is running.

|WDURATIONS|n

|y+powerinfo|n shows each power's duration in the book's words. The effect
list records one of three kinds: |yscene|n (most powers), |yturn|n (a number
of combat turns, advanced by staff) or |ypermanent|n (until removed).
Instant powers aren't recorded.

The game doesn't know when a scene ends, so a scene effect stays listed
after the scene; the Storyteller decides when it has ended.

|WWHAT AN EFFECT DOES|n

Fleetness adds your Celerity rating to noncombat Dexterity tests and one
Dexterity + Athletics defense per turn. Other defense pools receive no
Fleetness bonus. The best standard defense is chosen after modifiers.
Staff |y+effects/tick|n starts your next turn and renews that one defense;
using Fleetness again, logging out or clearing its record does not renew it.

Under the chronicle's provisional catalog policy, Prowess adds Potence to
Brawl/Melee attack damage, Toughness subtracts Fortitude from Superficial
physical damage, and Resilience adds Fortitude to your Health maximum.
Repeated activations do not stack these contributions. Toughness does not
reduce Aggravated damage. These catalog values await primary-source review.
Other recorded effects, such as being unseen or a Dominate command, remain
Storyteller-adjudicated unless a command explicitly describes a consumer.
Numeric |yroll <pool>|n commands have no trait or combat context: include
trait-specific bonuses and Health impairment in that pool with the
Storyteller. The game cannot infer Dexterity or a defense from a number.

Using a power again records a separate activation; automatic bonuses use
the strongest eligible contribution once, rather than adding duplicates.

|WCOMMANDS|n

|y+effects|n                     list your active effects
|y+effects/clear <id>|n          (staff) remove one of your own effects
|y+effects/clear all|n           (staff) remove all your own effects
|y+effects/tick|n                (staff) advance your turn-based effects by a turn

The staff switches act on the staff member's own character; there is no
switch yet to clear another character's effects.

Effects survive logging out and server reloads.
