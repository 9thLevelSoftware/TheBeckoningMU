|[R+===================================================================+
|W|                          BACKGROUNDS                              |
|[R+===================================================================+|n

Backgrounds represent resources, connections, and advantages your
character possesses (V5 core p.184-194; V5 Quick Reference 2.0 pp.8-11).
The ratings below are the book's. The |wBenefit|n and |wUses|n lines are
this game's conventions for the |y+background|n command, not book rules,
except Herd.

|WBACKGROUND LIST:|n

|yAllies|n (rated by effectiveness and reliability)
  Mortal associates, usually family or friends
  |wBenefit:|n Can call for help. +[dots] to Social rolls when relevant
  |wUses:|n [dots] times per session

|yContacts|n (dots 1-3)
  People who can get you information or items
  |wBenefit:|n +[dots] to Investigation when using contacts
  |wUses:|n [dots x 2] times per session

|yFame|n (dots 1-5: from a subculture to worldwide)
  How well known you are among mortals
  |wBenefit:|n +[dots] to Social rolls with those who recognize you
  |wUses:|n Unlimited (passive)

|yHaven|n (dots 1-3: from small and likely secure to large and private)
  A place to sleep safely by day
  |wBenefit:|n Security rating +[dots] to defend against intrusion
  |wUses:|n Passive

|yHerd|n (dots 1-5: from 1-3 mortals to 31-60)
  Mortals you can feed from freely and safely
  |wBenefit:|n Slake up to [dots] Hunger per week without a hunting roll
  (core p.189). Your Blood Potency still applies (see |yhelp hunger-guide|n).
  |wUses:|n Once per week (7 days from your last Herd feeding)

|yInfluence|n (dots 1-5: from well-connected to dominant)
  Political power in mortal society
  |wBenefit:|n +[dots] to Leadership/Politics in domain
  |wUses:|n [dots] times per session

|yMask|n (dots 1-2: a fake ID, or one that passes an in-depth check)
  A false identity
  |wBenefit:|n +[dots] to maintain Masquerade and resist investigation
  |wUses:|n Passive

|yMawla|n (dots 1-5: from a neonate to a prince or baron)
  A Kindred mentor, patron or confederate
  |wBenefit:|n Advice, protection and introductions
  |wUses:|n [dots] times per session

|yResources|n (dots 1-5: from paycheck to paycheck to ultra rich)
  Wealth, belongings and income
  |wBenefit:|n Can acquire items of [dots] rating or less
  |wUses:|n [dots] times per session

|yRetainers|n (dots 1-3: from a weak mortal to a gifted ghoul)
  Followers, guards and servants
  |wBenefit:|n [dots] servants who can perform tasks
  |wUses:|n Unlimited (they're always there)

|yStatus|n (dots 1-5: from known to a position of power)
  Standing in a sect
  |wBenefit:|n +[dots] to Social rolls with Kindred
  |wUses:|n Unlimited (passive)

Each background has matching flaws (Enemy, Infamy, Dark Secret, No Haven,
Obvious Predator, Disliked, Known Corpse, Adversary, Destitute, Stalkers,
Suspect, Shunned, ...).

|WUSING BACKGROUNDS:|n

|y+background|n
  View all your backgrounds, their benefit and uses left

|y+background <name>|n
  View details of a specific background
  Example: |y+background contacts|n

|y+background/use <name>=<task>|n
  Spend one use of a background on a task. The game records the use
  and tells you the bonus ([dots] dice); add it to your |yroll|n
  yourself when the Storyteller agrees it applies.
  Example: |y+background/use contacts=investigate gang activity|n

|y+background/herd|n
  Feed from your Herd (see Herd above). Lowers your Hunger at once.

|y+background/resources <item>=<rating>|n
  Record acquiring an item whose rating is at most your Resources;
  uses one Resources use. No object is created.
  Example: |y+background/resources pistol=2|n

|WIMPORTANT NOTES:|n

- Allies, Influence, Mawla and Resources have [dots] uses per session,
  Contacts [dots x 2]; the others are unlimited or passive, and Herd
  is weekly.
- Per-session uses don't refill on their own. |y+background/reset|n
  (staff) resets only the uses of the character running it, and no
  command yet resets another character's uses. The Herd week is never
  reset: it counts 7 days from your last Herd feeding.
- Backgrounds can be raised with XP (|y+spend advantage <name>|n).

|[r+===================================================================+|n

|yCommands:|n
  |w+background|n        - List your backgrounds
  |w+background/use|n    - Use a background
  |w+sheet|n             - View character (includes backgrounds)
