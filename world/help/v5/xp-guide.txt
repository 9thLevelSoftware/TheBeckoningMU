|[R+===================================================================+
|W|                     EXPERIENCE POINT SYSTEM                       |
|[R+===================================================================+|n

Experience points (XP) buy new dots. Staff award XP with +xpaward.

|WVIEWING XP|n

|y+xp|n           your current, earned and spent XP
|y+xp/log|n       your recent awards and purchases
|y+xp/costs|n     the cost table below

|WSPENDING XP|n

|y+spend attribute <name>|n                   e.g. +spend attribute strength
|y+spend skill <name>|n                       e.g. +spend skill brawl
|y+spend specialty <skill> = <specialty>|n   e.g. +spend specialty brawl = Grappling
|y+spend discipline <name> = <power>|n        e.g. +spend discipline Potence = Prowess
|y+spend/choices|n                            list pending historical power choices
|y+spend/claim <entitlement-id> = <power>|n   claim a historical choice without XP
|y+spend advantage <background or merit>|n   e.g. +spend advantage Resources
|y+spend advantage <background> = <who>|n    e.g. +spend advantage Allies = street gang
|y+spend bp|n                                 raise Blood Potency by one
|y+spend ritual <name>|n                      a Blood Sorcery ritual
|y+spend formula <name>|n                     a Thin-Blood Alchemy formula

Each spend buys one step. The name must be a real trait of that type
(+spend skill strength is refused), and nothing is charged when a spend is
refused.

A discipline dot and one eligible, previously unlearned power are bought
together. The power must belong to that discipline, fit the new rating,
and satisfy its catalog amalgam requirements. Omitting the power lists
eligible choices and spends nothing. Cost quotes remain available without
a choice. Thin-Blood Alchemy keeps its separate dot-plus-formula rule.

Staff can inventory historical dot-only purchases with the offline
reconcile_discipline_powers management command. Its default is a dry-run;
explicit --apply --offline issues choice entitlements only when retained
creation and XP history prove the deficit. Ambiguous records need staff
review. This never chooses a power or refunds XP. Claims consume a pending
choice once and charge no XP. A rejected application's replacement sheet
clears its old choices; a revoked character's retained sheet keeps them.

|WXP COSTS|n (V5 Quick Reference 2.0 p.1)

  Attribute                  new level x 5    (Strength 3 to 4: 20 XP)
  Skill                      new level x 3    (Brawl 2 to 3: 9 XP)
  Specialty                  3
  Clan Discipline            new level x 5
  Other Discipline           new level x 7
  Caitiff Discipline         new level x 6    (every discipline, for Caitiff)
  Blood Sorcery Ritual       ritual level x 3 (needs Blood Sorcery at that level)
  Thin-blood Formula         formula level x 3 (needs Thin-Blood Alchemy at that level)
  Thin-Blood Alchemy         new level x 5    (thin-bloods only; each dot comes with
                                               a formula: +spend discipline
                                               Thin-Blood Alchemy = <formula>)
  Advantage                  3 per dot        (backgrounds and merits; a 2-dot
                                               merit costs 6)
  Blood Potency              new level x 10   (up to your Generation's maximum:
                                               13th and 12th Generation: 3)

Willpower is Composure + Resolve: raise those attributes and it rises with
them. Humanity changes only at the Storyteller's discretion. Neither is
bought with XP. Thin-bloods can't buy other Disciplines (the Discipline
Affinity merit gives one permanent dot). Flaws aren't bought, and thin-blood merits are taken at
character creation.

To justify a new Discipline dot your character should feed on blood of the
matching Resonance; a new out-of-clan Discipline needs a taste of the blood
of someone who has it (QR p.1). Staff judge this.
