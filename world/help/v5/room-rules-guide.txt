ROOM POLICIES

The chronicle clock uses America/New_York. Day is 06:00 through 17:59;
night is 18:00 through 05:59. Day-only and night-only rooms restrict entry
for every character type. Existing exit and room locks still apply.

Restricted rooms use a reviewed list of character IDs. Staff receive no
automatic exception. An old restricted room with no clear list stays
closed until its policy is reviewed; a new publication requires the list.

A room's hunting modifier applies to whichever hunting ground you select.
A positive modifier reduces Difficulty once, to a minimum of 1; a negative
modifier increases it. The hunt result shows the contribution. Hunger,
feeding, resonance and the 24-hour cooldown retain their usual rules.

HAVENS

Haven security, size, luxury, warding and hidden-location ratings are facts
for Storyteller adjudication. They do not automatically grant bonuses,
capacity, concealed identity, successful intrusion or magical protection.

Ask staff to inspect the room's reviewed properties or the Haven facts
relevant to intrusion, space, comfort, occult protection or concealment.
Inspection does not roll or change outcomes.
