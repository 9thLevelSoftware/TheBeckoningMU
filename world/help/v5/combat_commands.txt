|y+attack <target>[=<attack pool>[/<weapon damage>]]|n
|y+attack <target>=<attack pool>/<weapon damage> vs <defense pool>|n   (staff)
  A contested attack. You roll your attack pool, one Attribute plus one
  Skill (default Strength + Brawl); the target rolls their best standard
  defense (Dexterity + Athletics, or Strength/Dexterity + Brawl/Melee
  against close combat). You hit with at least as many successes as the
  target; the damage is the margin (at least 1) plus the weapon damage.
  An Impaired attacker or defender loses 2 dice, and the pool shown is the
  pool rolled. The room sees both pools and the weapon. The attack only
  reports the damage: the Storyteller marks it with +damage.

  +attack Bob
  +attack Bob=Dexterity + Firearms/2
  +attack Bob=Strength + Melee/2 vs Strength + Melee     (staff)

|y+damage <amount>[/<type>]|n                (yourself)
|y+damage <target>=<amount>[/<type>]|n       (staff only for someone else)
|y+damage/unhalved <target>=<amount>|n       (staff: Superficial not halved)
  Types: superficial (default) or aggravated. A vampire halves mundane
  Superficial damage, rounding up. Reports Impairment (a full track) and
  torpor (a track full of Aggravated damage).

|y+heal|n
  Mend your own Superficial damage: one Rouse check heals your Blood
  Potency's mend amount. Refused at Hunger 5.

|y+heal <target>=<amount>[/<type>]|n         (staff only)
  Heal a character directly, with no Rouse check.

|y+health [<target>]|n
  Show a Health track: O healthy, / Superficial, X Aggravated.

|ySee also:|n help combat, help +stain, help mend
