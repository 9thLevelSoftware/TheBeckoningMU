|[R+===================================================================+
|W|                           COTERIES                                |
|[R+===================================================================+|n

A |ycoterie|n is a group of vampires who band together for mutual
benefit, protection and shared resources: the Kindred equivalent of a
crew. The game records the coterie, its members and four shared
resource ratings.

|WCOTERIE STRUCTURE:|n

|yLeader|n - the founder. Only the leader can invite, promote, demote and
  remove members, set resources and disband the coterie. A leader can't
  leave; they disband the coterie instead. There is no command to hand
  leadership to someone else.

|yLieutenant|n - a trusted member, promoted by the leader. The rank has no
  extra commands; what it means is up to the coterie and the Storyteller.

|yMember|n - a regular member.

|WCREATING A COTERIE:|n

|y+coterie/create <name>=<description>|n
  Found a new coterie; you become its leader. You can't already be in
  a coterie.

|yExample:|n
  +coterie/create Night Watch=Protectors of the Elysium

|WMANAGING MEMBERS:|n

|y+coterie/invite <character>|n        (leader only)
  Invite a character. They accept with |y+coterie/join <your name>|n.

|y+coterie/join <leader name>|n
  Accept a pending invitation from that leader.

|y+coterie/promote <character>|n       (leader only)
  Make a member a lieutenant.

|y+coterie/demote <character>|n        (leader only)
  Make a lieutenant a member again.

|y+coterie/kick <character>|n          (leader only)
  Remove a member.

|y+coterie/leave|n
  Leave your coterie (not the leader).

|y+coterie/disband|n                   (leader only)
  Disband the coterie; every member is removed.

|WRESOURCES:|n

|y+coterie/resources|n
  View the coterie's resource ratings.

|y+coterie/resource <type>=<value>|n   (leader only)
  Set a rating from 0 to 5. Types: domain, haven, herd, contacts.

|yExamples:|n
  +coterie/resource domain=3
  +coterie/resource herd=1

The ratings are a record for roleplay and for the Storyteller; the game
doesn't add them to rolls or feeding. Individual Backgrounds (such as
Herd or Contacts) are separate: see |yhelp backgrounds-guide|n.

  |wDomain|n   - territory the coterie controls
  |wHaven|n    - the quality and security of its shared havens
  |wHerd|n     - mortal feeding sources it has cultivated
  |wContacts|n - mortal informants and connections

|WVIEWING:|n

|y+coterie|n
  Your coterie and your role. The leader also sees its resources and
  the member list; members see the resources with |y+coterie/resources|n.

|y+social|n
|y+social <character>|n
  Status, boons and coterie membership in one display.

|WTIPS FOR COTERIE PLAY:|n

  - Choose members whose skills, clans and Disciplines complement
    each other.
  - Coordinate OOC so everyone benefits from membership.
  - Use the resources in your roleplay: meet in your Haven, lean on
    your Contacts.
  - Boons are owed between characters, not coteries, though a
    coterie can act together to repay one.
