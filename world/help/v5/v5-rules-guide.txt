TheBeckoningMU plays |yVampire: The Masquerade 5th Edition|n as the core
book and its Quick Reference (QR) write it. This page is the summary; the
other help files give the commands and the detail, and point back here.

|WDICE|n (QR p.3-4; help roll-guide)

  - A pool is usually |wAttribute + Skill|n d10s. Each 6-10 is a success.
  - Each |wpair of 10s|n is worth 4 successes: one 10 = 1, two = 4,
    three = 5, four = 8.
  - You succeed if your successes reach the Difficulty (at Difficulty 0
    you still need one). A |wcritical|n is a success with a pair of 10s.
  - |wWillpower re-roll|n: mark 1 Superficial Willpower damage to re-roll
    up to 3 regular dice of your last roll (|yroll/willpower|n).

|WHUNGER|n (QR p.4; help hunger-guide)

  - Hunger runs 0-5; a new vampire starts at 1. One die per point of
    Hunger is a |[RHunger die|n, up to the whole pool.
  - |wMessy critical|n: a critical where a Hunger die shows one of the
    10s. You succeed; the Storyteller decides the complication.
  - |wBestial failure|n: a failed roll with a Hunger die showing 1. You
    fail and act out a Compulsion.
  - |wRouse check|n: one die; 1-5 raises Hunger by 1. Powers, Blood Surge,
    mending and rising each night Rouse the Blood. The Hunger it costs is
    added after the action it fuels. At Hunger 5 you can't Rouse.
  - A Rouse that would take Hunger past 5 leaves it at 5 and calls for a
    hunger frenzy test (Difficulty 4) instead (help frenzy-guide).

|WBLOOD POTENCY|n (QR p.15; help blood_commands)

Blood Potency (0-10, capped by Generation) sets your Blood Surge dice, how
much one Rouse mends, a bonus to discipline pools, which Rouse checks you
may re-roll, your Bane Severity and how much each feeding slakes.

|WFEEDING|n (QR p.12; help hunt-guide)

|y+hunt <ground>|n rolls your Predator Type's hunting pool once every 24
hours; a win slakes 2 Hunger from a human. Staff-run hunt scenes
(|y+hunt/staffed|n) are the other way to feed. Only a kill takes Hunger
below 1, and high Blood Potency slakes less.

|WHUMANITY|n (QR p.3; help humanity-guide)

  - Humanity runs 0-10; new characters start at 7.
  - Breaking Chronicle Tenets or your Convictions marks |wStains|n.
  - At the end of a session, a |y+remorse|n test rolls one die per
    unmarked Humanity box (at least one). No success loses 1 Humanity;
    either way the Stains clear.
  - Each Conviction has a Touchstone. There is no other limit on
    Touchstones.

|WFRENZY|n (QR p.4, p.13; help frenzy-guide)

Resist with current |wWillpower + Humanity / 3|n against the provocation's
Difficulty (2-4). It is a Willpower test: no Hunger dice.

|WHEALTH, WILLPOWER AND DAMAGE|n (QR p.3, p.13; help combat)

  - Health = Stamina + 3; Willpower = Composure + Resolve.
  - Damage is |wSuperficial|n or |wAggravated|n. Vampires halve mundane
    Superficial damage (rounding up).
  - A full track is |wImpaired|n: -2 dice (Health: Physical tests;
    Willpower: Social and Mental tests).
  - A vampire whose Health track is full of Aggravated damage falls into
    torpor.
  - Attacks are contested rolls; staff mark the damage.

|WEXPERIENCE|n (QR p.1; help xp-guide)

XP buys new dots with |y+spend|n at the book's costs. Willpower and
Humanity can't be bought.

|WTHE SETTING|n

The Camarilla rules this city through the Prince and the Six Traditions
(the Masquerade, the Domain, the Progeny, the Accounting, Hospitality and
Destruction). Standing is |wStatus|n (help status-guide) and favors owed
are |wBoons|n (help boons-guide). The Second Inquisition hunts the Kindred,
and the Beckoning calls the elders away.
