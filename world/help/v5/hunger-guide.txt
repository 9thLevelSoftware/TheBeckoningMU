|[R+===================================================================+
|W|                        HUNGER SYSTEM                              |
|[R+===================================================================+|n

Hunger replaces the old Blood Pool. It measures how close the Beast is
(Quick Reference p.4). It runs from 0 to 5; a new vampire starts at 1.

|WHUNGER DICE:|n

Your |[RHunger dice|n replace regular dice in every roll, one per point of
Hunger, up to the whole pool. |yroll|n and |ypower|n use your current Hunger.

|yExample:|n Dexterity 3 + Stealth 2 = 5 dice at Hunger 2
  - You roll |W3 regular dice|n + |[R2 Hunger dice|n

  - Hunger dice count successes like any other die (6-10).
  - |yMessy critical:|n a critical (a successful roll with a pair of
    10s) where a Hunger die shows one of the 10s. You succeed; the
    Storyteller decides the complication.
  - |yBestial failure:|n a failed roll with any Hunger die showing 1.
    You fail and act out a Compulsion.
  - A Willpower re-roll never changes Hunger dice.

|WHUNGER 4 AND 5:|n

  - |yHunger 4+:|n you are prone to Hunger frenzy (see |yhelp frenzy-guide|n).
  - |yHunger 5:|n you can't Rouse the Blood. |yrouse|n, |ybloodsurge|n
    and any power that needs a Rouse check are refused until you feed.

|WINCREASING HUNGER:|n

Hunger rises by 1 each time you fail a |yRouse check|n (a 1-5 on one
die). You Rouse to:
  - use most discipline powers (some cost two or three checks)
  - |ybloodsurge|n
  - rise for the night, mend damage, or show the Blush of Life

The Hunger a Rouse costs is added after the action it fuels, so the
action's own roll uses your Hunger from before. Hunger never goes past
5: a failure that would take it higher means an immediate hunger frenzy
test (Difficulty 4).

Blood Potency lets you re-roll a failed Rouse check for discipline
powers up to a level set by the Blood Potency table (|yhelp blood_commands|n).

|yCommands:|n |yhunger|n shows your Hunger, resonance and any Blood Surge;
|yrouse [<reason>]|n makes a Rouse check.

|WDECREASING HUNGER (FEEDING):|n

Hunger falls when you feed:
  - |wMortals|n: the usual way; risks the Masquerade
  - |wAnimals and bagged blood|n: slake less, or nothing, at higher
    Blood Potency
  - |wOther vampires|n: risks blood bonds

Feed with |y+hunt|n (once a day), or ask for a staff-run scene with
|y+hunt/staffed|n; staff record the scene's feeding (see help hunt-guide).
