|W|                         V5 COMBAT                                 |
|[R+===================================================================+|n

Combat in V5 is a series of contested rolls (V5 core p.123-126). The
Storyteller frames each turn; the dice settle who gets hurt.

|WATTACKS ARE CONTESTED|n

The attacker rolls an attack pool (one Attribute plus one Skill) and the
defender rolls a defense pool, each with their own Hunger dice (mortals and
ghouls roll none):
  Unarmed: Strength + Brawl        Melee: Strength + Melee
  Ranged:  Dexterity + Firearms
The defender automatically rolls their best standard defense: Dexterity +
Athletics to dodge, or against a close-combat attack Strength or Dexterity
+ Brawl or Melee to fight back. Only staff choose another.

The attacker hits if they get at least as many successes as the defender
(a tie goes to the attacker). The damage is the |wmargin|n (attacker's
successes minus the defender's, at least 1: a tie does 1) plus the weapon's
damage, plus an active Potence bonus. An active Celerity bonus adds to the
defense. In a two-sided brawl the Storyteller applies the damage the
defender deals back.

|WDAMAGE|n

Health = Stamina + 3 boxes. Mark Superficial damage with / and
Aggravated damage with X (QR p.3).
  - |wVampires halve mundane Superficial damage|n, rounding up, before it is
    marked: 3 Superficial marks 2. Mortals, ghouls and thin-bloods
    (without the Vampiric Resilience merit) take it all.
  - Fire, sunlight, claws and some powers deal Aggravated damage, which is
    not halved. Fortitude's damage reduction applies to Superficial damage
    only.
  - When the track is full, each further Superficial point turns a
    Superficial box into Aggravated.

|WIMPAIRMENT AND TORPOR|n

  - A |wfull|n Health track (any mix of damage) is |wImpaired|n: -2 dice to
    Physical tests. A half-full track has no penalty. Spending Willpower
    ignores the penalty for a turn.
  - A full Willpower track is Impaired too: -2 dice to Mental and Social tests.
  - A Humanity tracker full of Stains (Degeneration) is Impaired: -2 dice to
    all tests until your Remorse test.
  - A vampire whose Health track is |wfull of Aggravated damage|n falls into
    torpor. A mortal in the same state is dying.

|WMENDING|n (QR p.13)

  - Superficial: once per turn, Rouse the Blood to mend damage equal to
    your Blood Potency's mend amount (BP 0-1: 1, 2-3: 2, 4-7: 3, 8-9: 4,
    10: 5). At Hunger 5 you can't Rouse, so you can't mend.
  - Aggravated: at nightfall, three Rouse checks heal 1 Aggravated damage
    (the Storyteller applies this).
  - Willpower: at the start of a session, heal Superficial Willpower damage
    up to your Composure or Resolve (whichever is higher).

|WWHO MARKS DAMAGE|n

+attack reports the damage; the Storyteller marks it with +damage. Players
may mark damage on themselves and mend their own Superficial damage with
+heal. Only staff can damage, heal or stain another character.
