|[R+===================================================================+
|W|                     THIN-BLOOD ALCHEMY                            |
|[R+===================================================================+|n

Thin-Blood Alchemy is the thin-bloods' answer to disciplines: formulas
distilled from blood and emotion that mimic vampiric powers. A thin-blood
gets it through the Thin-blood Alchemist merit. (V5 core p.282-288.)

|WIN THE BOOK:|n

  - Each formula is learned separately (XP: formula level x 3).
  - Distilling a formula costs 1 Rouse check, using one of three methods:
    |yAthanor Corporis|n (in your own body, Stamina + Alchemy),
    |yCalcinatio|n (in a mortal host, Manipulation + Alchemy) or
    |yFixatio|n (in a lab, Intelligence + Alchemy when it is used).
  - Using the formula then costs its own activation (usually 1 Rouse).
  - Each formula needs blood of particular Resonances.

|WCORE FORMULAS:|n

|yLevel 1:|n Far Reach (telekinesis), Haze (a cloud of mist)
|yLevel 2:|n Envelop (mist that blinds and smothers a target),
         Counterfeit (copy a discipline power one level lower; levels 2-5)
|yLevel 3:|n Defractionate (make bagged blood nourishing),
         Profane Hieros Gamos (reshape your body)
|yLevel 4:|n Airborne Momentum (flight)
|yLevel 5:|n Awaken the Sleeper (wake a vampire from day-sleep or torpor)

|WIN THE GAME:|n

|y+spend discipline Thin-Blood Alchemy = <formula>|n
  Buy an Alchemy dot (new level x 5 XP); each dot comes with one formula of
  that level or lower, named here.

|y+spend formula <name>|n
  Learn another formula (formula level x 3 XP; needs Thin-Blood Alchemy at its level)

|y+alchemy|n
  Your known formulas and distilled doses

|y+alchemy/distill <formula>[=<method>]|n
  Distil a dose: 1 Rouse check, and a roll of the method pool with your
  Hunger dice (athanor: Stamina, calcinatio: Manipulation, fixatio:
  Intelligence, each + Thin-Blood Alchemy). The difficulty is 3; only
  staff can set another (vs <difficulty>). A success gives one dose, and
  the room sees the distillation.
  Example: |y+alchemy/distill far reach=fixatio|n

|y+alchemy/use <formula>|n
  Use a dose: pay the formula's Rouse cost and roll its pool, if it has one.

At Hunger 5 you can't Rouse, so you can't distil or use a formula that
needs a Rouse check.

|y+alchemy/teach <character>=<formula>|n   (staff)
  Grant a formula without XP, e.g. the one the Thin-blood Alchemist merit
  gives.
