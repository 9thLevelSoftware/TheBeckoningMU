|[R+===================================================================+
|W|                 DISCIPLINE POWER COMMANDS                        |
|[R+===================================================================+|n

Commands for viewing and activating your vampire discipline powers.

|WVIEWING DISCIPLINES:|n

|y+disciplines|n
|y+disc|n
  View all your known disciplines and powers.

|y+disciplines <discipline name>|n
|y+disc <discipline name>|n
  View details of a specific discipline.

|yExamples:|n
  +disciplines                   # Show all disciplines
  +disciplines animalism         # Show Animalism powers
  +disc celerity                 # Show Celerity powers

|WACTIVATING POWERS:|n

|ypower <power name> [vs <difficulty>]|n
|ypower <power name> = <target>|n
|y+power <discipline>/<power name>|n
  Use a discipline power you know (+activate and +use also work).

|yExamples:|n
  power Scry the Soul vs 2
  power Awe = Bob
  +power animalism/bond famulus

Using a power will:
  1. Check you know it and have the discipline (and any amalgam) level
  2. Refuse it at Hunger 5 if it needs a |WRouse check|n
  3. Roll its pool, if it has one, with your current Hunger, adding the
     Blood Potency bonus and a die from a matching Intense or Acute
     resonance (and a readied Blood Surge); a named target rolls its
     resistance pool, and a tie goes to you
  4. Roll its |WRouse checks|n (0-3) with it, adding the Hunger they
     cost after the roll
  5. Apply effects if it succeeds (viewable with +effects)

See |yhelp dice_commands|n for details.

|WPOWER INFORMATION:|n

|y+powerinfo <discipline>/<power name>|n
|y+pinfo <discipline>/<power name>|n
  Get detailed information about a specific power.

|yExamples:|n
  +powerinfo animalism/bond famulus
  +pinfo celerity/cat's grace

Shows:
  - Power level requirement
  - Full description
  - Dice pool (if applicable)
  - Duration and effects
  - Whether you can currently use it

|WPOWER COSTS:|n

Each power lists how many |WRouse checks|n it costs (|y+powerinfo|n):
0 for free powers, usually 1, and 2 or 3 for a few. Each check is one
die: 6-10 nothing happens, 1-5 your Hunger rises by 1.

|WRESONANCE BONUSES:|n

If the blood you last fed on had an |wIntense|n or |wAcute|n resonance
(a Fleeting one gives no dice), you gain |g+1 die|n to powers of its
Disciplines:

  |ycholeric|n - Celerity, Potence powers
  |ymelancholy|n - Fortitude, Obfuscate powers
  |yphlegmatic|n - Auspex, Dominate powers
  |ysanguine|n - Blood Sorcery, Presence powers

|WACTIVE EFFECTS:|n

When powers create ongoing effects, use |y+effects|n to view them
(see |yhelp effects-guide|n).

|WHUNGER RESTRICTIONS:|n

At |[RHunger 5|n, you cannot use a power that needs a Rouse check.
Feed first. Below Hunger 5 you can start any power; if its failed
checks would take Hunger past 5, Hunger stops at 5 and you must test
for hunger frenzy (Difficulty 4).
