|W|                       THE CHRONICLE                                |
|[R+===================================================================+|n

The Chronicle contains staff-reviewed events from Athens by Night.

|WWEB PAGES:|n
  |y/chronicle/|n       - Public entries published by staff
  |y/chronicle/mine/|n  - Public entries plus participant-visible entries for your account

Drafts are not shown publicly. Participant entries are visible only to the accounts
that own the listed participant characters. Staff can correct or redact entries.

|WREAD-ONLY AGENT ACCESS:|n
An optional OAuth-protected MCP companion can read public lore, public Chronicle
entries, your own Chronicle, and summaries for your owned characters. It cannot
roll dice, change character state, publish stories, or run game commands. OAuth
authorization is scoped and revocable; never share access tokens.
