|[R+===================================================================+
|W|                     DICE ROLLING COMMANDS                        |
|[R+===================================================================+|n

Commands for rolling V5 dice pools.

|WBASIC ROLLING:|n

|yroll <pool> [vs <difficulty>]|n
|yr <pool> [vs <difficulty>]|n
  Roll a V5 dice pool. Your current Hunger sets the Hunger dice.

|yExamples:|n
  roll 7                         # Roll 7 dice
  roll 5 vs 3                    # 5 dice, 3 successes needed
  roll/secret 6 vs 2             # Only you see the result

|ySwitches:|n
  /secret   - Only show the result to you (no room broadcast)
  /willpower - Re-roll dice of your last roll (see below)

Staff only: |yroll <pool> <hunger>|n sets the Hunger dice, and
|yroll/mortal <pool>|n rolls with none (for NPCs).

|WWILLPOWER RE-ROLL:|n

|yroll/willpower <die> [<die> <die>]|n
  Mark 1 Superficial Willpower damage to re-roll up to three of your
  last roll's regular (non-Hunger) dice. Choose them by the value they
  show; you may re-roll a 10. Once per roll; it works after |ypower|n
  rolls too.

|yExample:|n
  roll/willpower 2 3 10          # Re-roll a 2, a 3 and a 10

|WDISCIPLINE POWERS:|n

|ypower <power name> [vs <difficulty>]|n
|ypower <power name> = <target>|n
|y+power <discipline>/<power name>|n
  Use a discipline power you know.

|yExamples:|n
  power Scry the Soul            # Pool calculated from your traits
  power Scry the Soul vs 3       # vs difficulty 3
  power Dread Gaze = Bob         # Contested: Bob rolls his resistance

The command:
  - Refuses a power you haven't learned or lack the rating for
  - Refuses a power that needs a Rouse check at Hunger 5
  - Builds the pool from your traits, plus the Blood Potency power
    bonus and a die from a matching Intense or Acute resonance
  - Rolls with your current Hunger, then makes the power's Rouse
    checks (some powers cost 2 or 3; free powers cost none)
  - Uses powers that have no dice roll without rolling
  - Starts the power's tracked effect, if it has one

Staff only: |ypower/norouse|n skips the Rouse checks.

|WROUSE CHECKS:|n

|yrouse [<reason>]|n
  Roll one die. On 6-10 nothing happens; on 1-5 your Hunger rises
  by 1. At Hunger 5 you can't Rouse the Blood. Discipline powers make
  their own Rouse checks (with the Blood Potency re-roll); a manual
  check gets no re-roll.

|WDICE MECHANICS REFERENCE:|n

|yshowdice|n
|yshowdice hunger|n
|yshowdice criticals|n
|yshowdice bestial|n
  Display the V5 dice rules.

|WV5 DICE RULES (Quick Reference p.3-4):|n

|ySuccess:|n
  - Each die is a d10
  - |g6-10|n = 1 success; 1-5 = none
  - Each |ypair of 10s|n = 4 successes (one 10 is 1, two are 4,
    three are 5, four are 8)
  - You succeed if your successes reach the difficulty (at least
    one success at difficulty 0)

|yCritical:|n
  - A successful roll with at least one pair of 10s

|yHunger Dice:|n
  - Replace regular dice, one per point of Hunger, up to the whole pool

|yMessy Critical:|n
  - A critical where a Hunger die shows one of the 10s
  - You succeed, but the Storyteller decides the complication

|yBestial Failure:|n
  - A failed roll with any Hunger die showing 1
  - You fail and act out a Compulsion
