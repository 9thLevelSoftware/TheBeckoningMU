|c[OCTOBER 2026] - RULES AND SYSTEMS UPDATE|n

The game now follows the V5 core book. What changed for players
(details in |whelp v5-rules-guide|n and the guides it points to):

|yDice and the Blood|n
- A lone 10 is one success; each |wpair of 10s|n is 4 successes
- Messy critical: a critical where a Hunger die shows one of the 10s.
  Bestial failure: a failed roll with any Hunger die showing 1
- Willpower re-roll: |wroll/willpower <die> [<die> <die>]|n re-rolls up
  to three regular dice of your last roll (|whelp roll-guide|n)
- Rouse checks are rolled with the action they fuel; the Hunger they
  cost is added afterwards. At Hunger 5 you can't Rouse the Blood
- |wbloodsurge <trait>|n readies a surge for your next |wroll|n or |wpower|n roll
- |wpower <name>|n uses a discipline power you know, with its pool,
  Rouse checks and effect (|whelp discipline_commands|n)

|yHumanity and Frenzy|n
- Remorse rolls one die per unmarked Humanity box (minimum one)
- The frenzy test is current Willpower + Humanity / 3, with no Hunger
  dice (|w+frenzy|n, |whelp frenzy-guide|n)
- A Rouse that would take you past Hunger 5 means a hunger frenzy test
  at Difficulty 4; |wrouse/wake|n rises for the night and
  |w+frenzy/pending|n rolls a test you still owe
- Touchstones go with Convictions; Humanity no longer caps them

|yDamage and Combat|n
- Vampires halve mundane Superficial damage (rounding up); you are
  Impaired only when your Health track is full
- |w+attack|n is contested: the target defends automatically, and
  staff mark the damage with |w+damage|n. Only staff can use
  |w+damage|n, |w+heal|n or |w+stain|n on someone else

|yFeeding and Advancement|n
- |w+hunt <ground>|n rolls your predator type's hunting pool against the
  hunting ground's difficulty, once every 24 hours; |w+hunt/staffed|n
  asks staff for a hunt scene (|whelp hunt-guide|n)
- |w+spend <type> <name>|n uses the V5 core XP costs (|w+xp/costs|n)
- Thin-Blood Alchemy: |w+alchemy/distill|n and |w+alchemy/use|n; formulas
  need no ingredients

|ySocial Systems|n
- Boons: the debtor offers (|w+boongive|n), the creditor accepts
  (|w+boonaccept|n) and calls it in (|w+booncall|n), both confirm it
  repaid (|w+boonfulfill|n); |w+boon/dispute|n goes to a Harpy or staff
- Jobs are named |w<bucket>/<number>|n (e.g. |w+job Hunt Scenes/3|n);
  file one with |wjob/submit <bucket> <title> = <description>|n

|yCharacters and Help|n
- Characters are created only on the website (|w/character-creation/|n)
  and played once staff approve them (|whelp chargen|n)
- Long guides in the help system now end in |w-guide|n (|whelp roll-guide|n,
  |whelp hunger-guide|n, ...) so they no longer hide behind commands
