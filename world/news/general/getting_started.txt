|cSTEP 2: LEARN THE BASICS|n

|yEssential Commands|n

  |whelp|n               - Access the help system
  |w+sheet|n             - View your character sheet
  |w+st|n                - One-line vitals (Hunger, Health, Willpower)
  |wwho|n                - See who's online
  |wlook|n               - Look at your surroundings
  |winventory|n          - Check your inventory

|yCommunication|n

  |wpage <name>=<msg>|n  - Send a private message to a player
  |w+bbs|n               - List the bulletin boards
  |w+bbread <board>/<#>|n - Read a post
  |w+bbpost <board>=<title>/<body>|n - Post to a board
  |wsay <text>|n         - Speak in the current room
  |wpose <action>|n      - Describe your actions

|cSTEP 3: UNDERSTAND V5 MECHANICS|n

|yCore Systems|n (see |whelp v5-rules-guide|n)

  |wroll <pool> [vs <difficulty>]|n - Roll dice (e.g. |wroll 5 vs 2|n)
  |wrouse|n              - Make a Rouse check
  |w+hunt <ground>|n     - Hunt and feed (once every 24 hours)
  |w+hunt/staffed <ground>|n - Ask staff for a hunt scene
  |whunger|n             - Check your Hunger
  |w+humanity|n          - View Humanity, Stains, Convictions

|yDisciplines (Vampire Powers)|n

  |w+disciplines|n       - List your disciplines and powers
  |wpower <name>|n       - Use a discipline power you know
  |w+powerinfo <discipline>/<power>|n - Details of a power

|yCombat (when needed)|n

  |w+attack <target>|n   - Contested attack; the target defends automatically
  |w+health|n            - View your Health track
  |w+heal|n              - Mend Superficial damage with a Rouse check
  Staff mark damage with |w+damage|n (see |whelp combat|n).

|cSTEP 4: JOIN THE COMMUNITY|n

|yFind Roleplay|n

  - Check |wwho|n to see who's online
  - Read |w+bbs general|n for RP announcements
  - Visit public gathering locations
  - Join or form a Coterie with |w+coterie|n

|yUnderstand Status|n

  - Check your Status with |w+status|n
  - Status represents political standing in vampire society
  - Higher Status = more influence in Camarilla affairs

|yTrack Boons|n

  - Boons are favors owed between vampires; check yours with |w+boon|n
  - The debtor offers: |w+boongive <character> <type> = <description>|n
  - The creditor accepts (|w+boonaccept|n) and later calls it in
    (|w+booncall|n); both confirm repayment with |w+boonfulfill|n
  - Disputes (|w+boon/dispute|n) are ruled on by a Harpy or staff
    (see |whelp boons-guide|n)

|cKEY CONCEPTS FOR NEW PLAYERS|n

|yThe Hunger|n
- Ranges from 0 (satiated) to 5 (ravenous)
- Rises when a Rouse check fails (Disciplines, Blood Surge, mending)
- Feed with |w+hunt|n (once every 24 hours) or in a staff-run scene
- At Hunger 5 you can't Rouse the Blood until you feed

|yThe Masquerade|n
- NEVER reveal your vampire nature to mortals
- Violations = Blood Hunt (other vampires hunt you)
- Use Disciplines discreetly
- Clean up after feeding

|yHumanity & Stains|n
- Humanity measures your hold on the Beast (0-10)
- Stains accumulate from immoral acts
- Make your Remorse test at session end with |w+remorse|n
- Lose 1 Humanity if it scores no successes

|yFrenzy|n
- The Beast taking control
- Three types: fury, hunger or terror (fire and sunlight cause terror);
  |w+frenzy/check <type>|n lists each type's provocations
- Resist with |w+frenzy/resist <difficulty>|n
- Consequences can be severe
